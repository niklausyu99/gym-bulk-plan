# BulkPlan

## 1. Visual Theme & Atmosphere

BulkPlan is a daily iPhone training and nutrition app. On 2026-11-01 the user explicitly replaced the prior paper/sage/navy direction with a modern black/charcoal/lime gym theme. Use pure black ground, charcoal surface steps and fit-lime highlights. Muscle-location teaching uses a shared charcoal scene with neutral gray anatomical figures and deterministic regional tinting; food illustrations use the existing watercolor treatment. Keep the functional layout and data behavior while applying the newly requested palette.

## 2. Color

Use tokens in app/theme.css: ground #000000, paper #1c1c1c, panel #262626, ink #f5f5f2, muted #b8b8b0, accent #affa01, supporting lime #cfed89, line #3b3b3b, negative #ff9891. The reference image has inconsistent printed hex labels; its actual swatches guide these values. Color marks selection, action and meaning. Keep primary, assisting and stabilizing muscle roles explicit in text; highlighting is not a measured activation map.

Muscle highlighting is part of the required teaching asset, not an optional polish step. Neutral-gray renders are production intermediates, not complete replacements. Respect visibility: do not color the wrong visible muscle to represent an occluded target. Keep one shared front/back neutral base for all exercises. Deep muscles use explicitly identified translucent position projections; do not claim they are exposed surface anatomy. Orange marks primary targets; pale gold can mark assisting muscles; muted teal marks stabilizing regions. Muted red dashed outlines show conditional control risks only; never infer compensation from normal participation. Shades mark qualitative emphasis, not measured activation.

## 3. Typography

Preserve the system stack beginning Avenir Next, Hiragino Sans and PingFang SC. Mobile input text is at least16px. Use tabular numerals for counts, weights and timers. Titles name the user's task; no slide numbers, production labels or development terminology in product UI.

## 4. Spacing & Grid

Design for the iPhone first, with 16px content margins and touch targets at least44px. The header, scrolling content, timer/status dock and five-item navigation own separate shell rows. Respect safe areas and the onscreen keyboard. Wider layouts can use the existing12-column grid.

## 5. Layout & Composition

Today starts with the next workout or existing draft. Training supports one set at a time. Nutrition starts with actual day/Blend choices. Progress uses real records. More supplies the action library, product handbook, installation and backup. Do not add a landing-page hero or marketing funnel inside daily use.

## 6. Components

Preserve explicit selected states, visible form labels and destructive-action confirmation. Numeric steppers show minus and plus. Exercise details contain a neutral muscle-location diagram with a readable role legend, a large-image entrance and concise setup, movement-range and breathing text. User-provided exercise animations are now requested again. Only visually verified animations for the exact selected equipment version may appear; no approximate exercise substitutions or pose hotspots. Keep image labels outside the body and physically distinct.

## 7. Motion & Interaction

Motion responds to an action: a changed selection, saved set or opened muscle diagram. Exercise demonstrations loop silently and inline without playback controls, as explicitly requested. No unrelated autoplay or scroll presentation. Preserve immediate action feedback, reduced-motion support and cleanup when rendering changes. Muscle-location maps do not demonstrate an exercise trajectory. GSAP Skills are available for future sequencing; simple transitions can remain CSS.

## 8. Voice & Brand

Use concise Chinese suitable for a person who has never seen the project conversation. Explain what to do, the expected movement range, breathing and relevant limits. Keep actual nutrition uncertainty, data ownership and recovery signals only where they affect a decision. Source archives, prompts, audits, versions, production blueprints and implementation notes stay in developer files and are excluded from the app package.

## 9. Anti-patterns

No Markdown/blueprint framing, design instructions, source-difference reports, development-server instructions, fabricated personal records, generated quality scores, multi-exercise atlases, cropped bodies or automatically inferred compensation. The user explicitly authorized the black/lime redesign and targeted review. Preserve training/nutrition content and verified data behavior; no marketing content inside daily screens.
